# Account, Billing and Invoices

## Invoices
An invoice is generated automatically for every paid order and emailed to the billing
address on the account. Customers can download past invoices from "My Orders" at any
time. Support can resend an invoice to the account's registered email address.

## Payment methods
Acme accepts major credit and debit cards, PayPal and bank transfer for business
customers. Card details are stored only by the payment provider; support agents can
never see or request full card numbers.

## Subscriptions
Filter subscriptions renew every 6 months. Customers can pause, skip or cancel a
subscription from the account page up to 3 days before the renewal date. Cancelling
after a renewal has been charged is treated as a normal return.

## Password reset and account security
Password resets are self-service through the "Forgot password" link. Support never asks
for a password, one-time code or full card number by email. Suspicious emails asking for
such details should be reported to security@acme.example.

## Changing account details
The email address on an account can be changed from the profile page after confirming the
new address. Support can update the postal address and phone number on request when the
customer writes from the registered email address.
