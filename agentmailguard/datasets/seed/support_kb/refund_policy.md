# Acme Home Appliances - Refund and Return Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the delivery date for unused
items returned in their original packaging. Items showing signs of use may receive a
partial refund of up to 70% at the discretion of the returns team.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or stops working within the first 30 days, Acme covers the
return shipping and offers the customer a choice between a replacement unit and a full
refund. Customers should keep the packaging and, where possible, send a photo of the damage.

## How refunds are issued
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the
returned item is received at the warehouse. Bank processing may add up to 3 business days.
Gift-card purchases are refunded as store credit.

## Exclusions
Consumables (filters, bags, cleaning solution), clearance items marked "final sale" and
personalised products are not eligible for return unless they arrived defective.

## Starting a return
Customers start a return from the "My Orders" page by selecting the order and clicking
"Return item". A prepaid label is generated automatically for damaged or defective items.
Support agents can also start a return on the customer's behalf using the order number.
