# X200 Cordless Vacuum - Product Information

## Specifications
The X200 is a cordless stick vacuum with a 25.2 V lithium-ion battery, up to 60 minutes
of runtime in eco mode and 12 minutes in boost mode. Charging takes about 4 hours. The
dust bin holds 0.8 litres and the HEPA filter is washable.

## Common questions
A rattling noise usually means debris is caught in the brush roll; remove the roll and
clear any hair or hard objects. A flashing red light indicates the filter needs cleaning
or the bin is full. If the battery drains in under 10 minutes on eco mode after a full
charge, the battery may need replacement, which is covered under warranty within 24 months.

## Accessories and spare parts
Replacement filters (part F-200), brush rolls (part BR-200) and batteries (part BAT-200)
are available in the online store. Filters should be washed monthly and replaced every
6 months.

## Pricing
The X200 retails at 249 USD. Education and non-profit customers receive a 10% discount on
orders of 10 units or more when ordering through the sales team.

## Safety
Do not use the X200 to pick up liquids, hot ash or sharp objects. Only charge with the
supplied adapter.
