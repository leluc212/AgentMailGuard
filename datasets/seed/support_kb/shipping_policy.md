# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the country and 7-14 business days for
international orders. Express delivery (1-2 business days) is available at checkout for
orders placed before 14:00.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking updates
can take up to 24 hours to appear on the carrier's website.

## Delayed or lost parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, support
opens a carrier investigation. Investigations take up to 10 business days. If the parcel
is confirmed lost, Acme sends a replacement or issues a full refund, at the customer's choice.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed free of charge until the order status is "Packed".
After that, the carrier may allow a redirect for a fee.

## Shipping costs
Standard delivery is free for orders above 50 USD. Below that threshold a flat fee of
5.90 USD applies. Express delivery costs 12.90 USD regardless of order value.
